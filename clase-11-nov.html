Warm up · preguntas detonadoras 4 min

Function: apologizing and responding to apologies 6 min

Student Text · página 95 · Look at the chart.
Para disculparse
  • I’m sorry that I’m late.
  • I’m late. Please excuse me.
  • Forgive me for being late.
  • I apologize for being late.
Para responder a una disculpa
  • That’s okay. Hope you are more careful in the future.
  • There’s no problem.
  • Don’t worry about it. But be more careful next time.
  • That’s all right.
  • I accept your apology. / I cannot accept your apology.

Fíjate en la última: I cannot accept your apology también es una respuesta válida. No toda disculpa se acepta.

Reading: identifying the topic and main idea 7 min

Student Text · página 97 · Read the paragraph, write the topic, and select or write the main idea.

El tema son dos o tres palabras: de qué trata. La idea principal es una oración completa: qué dice el párrafo sobre ese tema.

Paragraph 1
Military bases are like small cities. The offices and buildings are similar to the offices and buildings in a town. There are houses on base for officers and enlisted people. There are also schools for the children of those people who live on the base. There are gyms where you can work out and places to play tennis, football, and baseball. The commissary is like a large grocery store. The BX usually has just about anything else that you need. Most bases also have hospitals to take care of anyone who is sick. There’s also a gas station and a garage to repair your car. Last, most bases have buses and taxis to take you where you need to go if you don’t have a car.

1What is the topic of this paragraph?

✓ military bases

2What is the main idea of this paragraph?

✓ a) Military bases and cities are similar. — todo el párrafo compara la base con una ciudad; lo demás son solo detalles.

Paragraph 2
Almost all the people in the world use some kind of money to buy the things they need. In the past, rocks, shells, animals, animal’s teeth, and cigarettes were used as money. Today, coins and paper bills are used as money in most places. Coins are usually small and round with words, numbers, and pictures on them. Bills come in different sizes and colors and also have pictures, words, and numbers. In the US, bills used to be green, but now they are using different colors to make the paper money.

1What is the topic of this paragraph?

✓ money

2What is the main idea of this paragraph? (esta se escribe, no se elige)

✓ People have used different kinds of money, and today most places use coins and bills.

Exercise C de la p. 96 — el producto final de la lección

Escriban y presenten su diálogo 15 min

Student Text · página 96 · Work with a partner. Choose one situation. Write a dialog and say it to the class.
1You’re at a party. You spill your drink on someone.
2You broke something at Helen’s house.
3You forgot to pick up the pizza on your way home from work.
4You forgot to bring your homework to class.
5You bought milk and eggs for your mother. You forgot to buy bread.
6It’s your mother’s birthday. You forgot to call her.
Para que cuente como diálogo completo:
  1. Una disculpa de las del cuadro de la p. 95.
  2. Decir por qué, con for + -ing o con that.
  3. Una respuesta del cuadro, y cerrar el intercambio.

Se presenta ante el grupo. Se evalúa con la lista de cotejo del 11 de noviembre, siete criterios: ustedes mismos, otra pareja y el profesor.
